Where the time of a request through a kith Service goes: one run, three kith OS machines
(QEMU with KVM, 2 CPUs, 2048 MB), one nginx, `ab -n 20000 -c 20` on the first node.

    KITH_COMPARE_ONLY=web,where KITH_COMPARE_RAW=FILE sh ci/compare.sh --only kith --runs 1

The row it wrote (rps: a new connection for each request; kept: connections that are
kept; p50 and p99 in ms). No prefix: the Service in front of three nginx, as in
ci/compare.txt. direct_: the web server asked on its own node, no Service between.
near_: through its Service on that node (kithd passes the bytes once). lan_: the web
server on another node, asked on the nodes' own network. mesh_: the same over the mesh
(WireGuard). far_: through its Service from another node (two kithds and the mesh).
